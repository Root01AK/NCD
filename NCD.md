Section 14 · Linkage and follow-up tracking    —  Case management coordinator
How the three-attempt engine works. All three attempt dates generate automatically from the screening date: attempt 1 at day 15, attempt 2 at day 30, attempt 3 at day 45. The coordinator records what actually happened at each attempt. Once linkage is confirmed the remaining attempts close automatically. If all three are exhausted without linkage, the record closes as lost to follow-up, a reason code becomes compulsory, and the system does not permit a fourth attempt or any date extension.

Note for the database developer
Build requirements, not questionnaire content. Read alongside the instrument.
No free text anywhere. Every analytic field is a coded option, a number, or a date. Do not add comment boxes at any stage and do not allow an "other" value to be typed. Residual codes carry no write-in box.
Automatic follow-up scheduling. On saving the screening record, generate three attempt dates from the screening date: day 15, day 30, day 45. These are system-generated and cannot be edited by the user.
Hard stop after three attempts. Once attempt 3 is recorded without linkage, the record closes as lost to follow-up. The system must not permit a fourth attempt, a new date, or an extension. The reason code at Q104 becomes compulsory and the record cannot be saved without it.
Early close on linkage. If any attempt returns "linked," close the remaining attempts automatically and open the linkage confirmation fields.
Monthly loss-to-follow-up pull. On the first working day of each month, run an automatic extract of all records that closed as lost to follow-up in the preceding month, broken down by site, reason code and referral reason, and flag it to the programme manager without anyone having to request it. Also flag any record where an attempt date has passed with no outcome entered, since those are silent drop-outs rather than recorded ones.
Compulsory safety validation. The form cannot be submitted where PHQ-9 item 9 (Q64) is positive and the escalation field (Q65) is blank.
Range checks at entry. Apply plausibility ranges to every numeric field, in particular waist-hip ratio (0.60 to 1.40), BMI (10 to 60), systolic BP (70 to 260), diastolic BP (40 to 160), RBS (30 to 600) and haemoglobin (3 to 20). Reject out-of-range values at entry, not at analysis.
Auto-calculated fields. BMI, waist-hip ratio, average blood pressure, Heaviness of Smoking Index, AUDIT-C total and the Amber review date are calculated, not typed.
Exclusive codes. Where a "none of the above" code exists in a check-all question, selecting it must clear and block all other selections.
Facility lookup table. Hold a separate reference table mapping each facility code to the named facility at each site, so the coded field stays stable if a facility name changes.
Separate clinical card. Clinicians needing narrative detail record it on the participant's clinical card, which is not part of the analytic database and is not extracted.
Four points to confirm before the build starts
Loss-to-follow-up code 9. You asked for nine reasons and listed eight. I have added "could not be contacted after three attempts" as code 9. Confirm.
Random blood sugar and haemoglobin. Read literally, dropping biochemistry would remove these two point-of-care tests. Without random blood sugar there is no diabetes detection and the Amber and Red bands cannot function. I have kept both and dropped only the laboratory panels. Confirm.
Oral cavity examination. Retained as part of the routine clinical examination, including precancerous lesions, which matters given tobacco use in these communities. It is not a cancer screening programme. Confirm you are content.
Interview length. This version runs to 117 questions, though skip logic means most participants answer far fewer. Someone who has never used tobacco, alcohol or other substances and screens negative on mental health skips roughly 30. Please pilot for timing before full deployment.
